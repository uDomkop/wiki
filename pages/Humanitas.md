# Humanitas
```
Perhaps I've let humanity defy me too much..
Humanity's mind, its passions.
Love, Hate. Warmth and Cold.

I want to be the one on the wind.
Yet still the one that acts when asked.
And be free. And yet together.
Be myself as part of a whole.
Not just a pawn.
Yet freely a pawn.
Move to move as you see fit.
Within my comfort.
Set forth Humanitas.
```
- deDomkop
